How It Works

1

Install & Setup

Add Escape Button to your browser. Configure your safe site URL and whitelist the sites you want to keep in your history.

2

Press the Button

When you need to escape, press Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Cmd+Shift+Z on Mac) anywhere in your browser.

3

Complete Protection

All tabs close instantly. Your safe site opens. Your history is cleared except for whitelisted sites. You're safe.
