How It Works

1

Install & Configure

Add the extension to Chrome. Set your safe site URL and choose which sites to whitelist in the options.

2

One Keystroke

When you need to escape, press Ctrl+Shift+Z (or Cmd+Shift+Z on Mac) anywhere in your browser.

3

Instant Protection

All tabs close, your safe site opens in a new tab, and your history is cleared (except whitelisted sites).
